Residential / Northern coast

Hillside Villas

StatusIn progress
Year2026
TypeResidential
Homes14 villas
Area6,200 m²
The idea

Fourteen homes step down a west-facing slope, each one turned a few degrees so no terrace looks into another.

Long horizontal roofs shade the glass through the afternoon and collect the evening light. Local stone anchors the lower floors to the hill.

Cars stay at the top of the site. Everything below it is paths, gardens and water.
