Security in four layers.

Who gets in, what they can do, and where your data can go.

  1. Authorization

    Access down to a single permission.

    • 67 permissions. Roles only set the defaults.
    • Grant or revoke one, for one person.
    • Nobody hands out more than they hold.

  2. Authentication

    Only invited people get in.

    • Invitation only. No public sign-up.
    • Access tokens last 15 minutes.
    • A stolen session ends every session.

  3. Encryption

    Encrypted in transit, private in storage.

    • HTTPS only, enforced by the browser.
    • Passwords kept only as salted hashes.
    • File links expire within minutes.

  4. AI privacy

    The AI sees only what it must.

    • Salaries and bank details never reach it.
    • It cannot write while it works.
    • It sees only what the asker may see.
